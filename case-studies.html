Case Study 01Retail

Enterprise Change Management Strategy, Capability Development and Delivery for a 30,000-Associate International Retail Group

The Challenge: A leading international retail group with over 30,000 associates was navigating unprecedented change: rapid acquisitions, shifting customer demands, digital transformation opportunities, and supply chain disruption — all simultaneously.

The organization lacked a consistent enterprise approach to managing change, resulting in change fatigue, poor adoption rates, and unrealized transformation benefits.

Our Approach: Global Apex worked alongside the executive team to develop a comprehensive enterprise change management strategy, establish consistent methodology, and build internal capability to sustain it over the long term.

Scope

30,000+ associates across multiple countries

Leaders Trained

200+ executives and senior managers

Outcome

Enterprise change readiness significantly improved

Case Study 02Pharma

Integration Planning and Post-Merger Integration for a $4 Billion Acquisition at a Fortune 200 Biopharmaceutical Company

The Challenge: A Fortune 200 biopharmaceutical company closed a $4 billion acquisition with an aggressive integration timeline, requiring immediate cross-functional coordination across dozens of workstreams, geographies, and business units.

Our Approach: Global Apex established a complete cross-functional integration plan within 10 days of deal close — providing the structure, processes, and change management expertise to ensure a seamless post-merger integration.

We developed a reusable integration playbook for future M&A activity, embedding lasting capability within the organization.

Deal Value

$4 billion acquisition

Plan Established

Within 10 days of deal close

Outcome

On-time deal close & seamless integration
